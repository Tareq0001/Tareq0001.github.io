1. System Architecture & Digital Twin Scope
The PACK-SILO industrial SCADA suite delivers real-time digital twin monitoring and fail-safe automation logic for a modern cement manufacturing dispatch terminal. It seamlessly bridges raw storage inventory, automated bag packaging, and bulk tanker logistics with microsecond event chronology.
Controller: Siemens S7-1517F-3 PN/DP Fail-Safe PLC Network: Redundant Profinet Ring (MRP) + IO-Link for Digital Sensors Motor Management: Siemens SIMOCODE Pro V via Profinet IO Weighing Electronics: OIML R61 Class X(1) Net-Weighing Controllers
2. Cement Silo Aeration & 80GHz FMCW Radar Level Gauging
Four 15,000 Ton steel/concrete silos (OPC, SRC, PPC, Finishing) utilize high-frequency 80 GHz FMCW (Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave) level radars. The narrow 3° beam angle avoids silo internal cross-bracing and dust buildup errors. Bottom fluidization is driven by positive displacement Roots rotary lobe blowers (400V - 90 kW - 0.8 Bar) injecting air through permeable polyester air pads to prevent powder clumping and bridging.

3. Haver & Boecker 8-Spout Rotary Packer & Net-Weighing Algorithm
The rotary packaging carousel features 8 radially oriented filling spouts with dedicated high-speed digital strain gauge load cells. Dosing executes in a three-stage precision cycle:
Stage 1: Tare & Bag Verification (Pneumatic saddle clamp engaged) Stage 2: Coarse Filling (Impeller high RPM, proportional pinch valve 100% open, 0 -> 45.0 kg in 1.4s) Stage 3: Fine Dosing (Proportional pinch valve throttles to 15%, fine pulse feed, 45.0 -> 50.00 kg ± 0.15 kg) Discharge: Ultrasonic valve bag sealing, clamp release, and kicker arm drops bag onto BC-201 conveyor
4. Dynamic Checkweigher & High-Speed Pneumatic Rejector Interlock
Downstream belt conveyor BC-201 feeds into dynamic inline checkweigher CW-201. Any bag exceeding the statutory legal tolerance (<49.85 kg or >50.15 kg), or flagged with a ruptured bag ultrasonic sensor, triggers the high-speed pneumatic pusher PR-201 (0.8 MPa compressed air piston) within 45 ms, ejecting the out-of-spec bag into a dedicated recycle hopper while allowing compliant bags to proceed to telescopic loader TL-101.
5. Bulk Tanker Weighbridge Cutoff & ATEX Grounding Permissive
The 60.0 Ton ground weighbridge features an automated cutoff interlock. Before the telescopic bellows BL-301 can lower into the tanker manhole, the ATEX intrinsically safe electrostatic grounding clamp must achieve a loop resistance < 10 Ω. Once the target payload (e.g. 32.50 Tons) is reached, the pneumatic slide gate valve slams shut in 280 ms to prevent overfilling.
6. Edge AI Predictive Maintenance & Vibration Spectral Signature
High-frequency accelerometers on the Bucket Elevator BE-201 upper bearings and continuous load cell drift tracking on the 8 spouts feed an on-premise Weibull Hazard Rate algorithm. Early bearing wear and load cell creep are detected dozens of operating hours before catastrophic failure, preserving uptime and preventing unmonitored dispatch errors.
