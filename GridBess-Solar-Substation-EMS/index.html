1. 150 MWp Utility-Scale Solar PV & Irradiance Conversion

The photovoltaic array consists of bifacial monocrystalline silicon modules configured on single-axis horizontal trackers with astronomical backtracking algorithm.
Active Power Generation Model: P_pv = P_mp_stc × (G_poa / 1000) × [1 + γ × (T_cell - 25)] × η_inv × η_mismatch Where γ = -0.34%/°C (temperature coefficient), η_inv = 98.9% (transformerless central inverter efficiency).
Grid Reactive Power Support (Grid Code Compliance): Q_limit = ± √(S_inv² - P_pv²) = ± 45 MVAR (Dynamic Voltage Support at 33 kV Point of Interconnection)

2. 200 MWh LFP BESS & Primary Frequency Droop Control (60.00 Hz)

The Battery Energy Storage System utilizes Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) cells arranged in 16 racks per container across 4 primary enclosures, coupled to 100 MW bidirectional 4-quadrant Power Conversion Systems (PCS).
Primary Frequency Droop Characteristic: ΔP = - (1 / R) × [(f_grid - f_nominal) / f_nominal] × P_rated Where R = 4.0% droop, f_nominal = 60.00 Hz, deadband = ±0.02 Hz (59.98 to 60.02 Hz). Fast Frequency Response Time: t_response ≤ 120 ms (Inverter PWM synthetic inertia).

3. 132/33 kV Substation & Transformer Differential Protection (ANSI 87T)

Step-up transformer rated at 160 MVA ONAN/ONAF, Dyn11 vector group. Primary protection is provided by numerical percentage-restrained differential relay with harmonic restraint.
Dual-Slope Restrained Differential Equation: I_diff = |I_1 + I_2|  |  I_rest = (|I_1| + |I_2|) / 2 Trip Condition: I_diff > Slope_1 × I_rest + I_pickup (for I_rest < I_knee) Slope 1 = 20%, Slope 2 = 60%, I_pickup = 0.20 pu. Operates within 18 ms to isolate 132kV and 33kV breakers simultaneously.

4. Online Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) & Duval Triangle Diagnostics

Continuous monitoring of transformer dielectric oil using photoacoustic spectroscopy to detect combustible gases: Hydrogen (H2), Methane (CH4), Ethylene (C2H4), and Acetylene (C2H2).
GAS SPECIES NORMAL LIMIT ALERT LEVEL DIAGNOSTIC INDICATION
Hydrogen (H2) < 50 ppm > 100 ppm Partial discharge & corona in oil
Acetylene (C2H2) < 1.0 ppm > 2.0 ppm High energy thermal arcing between windings
BESS Cell ΔV Spread < 25 mV > 150 mV Impending cell internal short / thermal runaway

5. Automatic Synchrocheck (ANSI 25) Reclosure Window

Enforces permissives prior to closing 132 kV breaker CB-101 to prevent out-of-phase mechanical shock to the generator and transformer shafts:
ΔV = |V_bus - V_line| / V_rated ≤ 5.0% Δf = |f_bus - f_line| ≤ 0.10 Hz Δθ = |θ_bus - θ_line| ≤ 10.0 degrees (Holding for 500 ms)
